Internet and Networking Topics

Everything you need to know to get started, explained simply.

1. What Is Computer Networking?

Computer networking is the practice of connecting computers and other devices, such as phones, printers, and tablets, so they can communicate and share information.

Devices can be connected using cables or wirelessly (Wi-Fi). Once connected, they can share files, share an internet connection, and use the same printer.

2. Types of Networks

Networks are grouped by how large an area they cover.

PAN

Personal Area Network. Connects devices within about a few meters of one person.

Example: a phone connected to Bluetooth earphones.

LAN

Local Area Network. Connects devices in one small area like a home or school.

Example: the computers in a school computer lab.

MAN

Metropolitan Area Network. Covers a whole city or large town.

Example: a city-wide network linking government offices.

WAN

Wide Area Network. Covers a very large area, such as countries or the world.

Example: the internet.

3. Networking Devices

📶

Router

Sends data between networks and directs it to the right device. It connects your home to the internet.

🔀

Switch

Connects many devices in the same network, like computers in a lab, and sends data to the correct one.

📠

Modem

Converts signals from your internet provider so your network can use the internet.

🔌

Network Cable

Carries data between devices. A common type is the Ethernet cable.

4. Network Topologies

A topology is the way devices in a network are arranged and connected.

⭐ Star

All devices connect to one central device like a switch. If one cable fails, the others keep working.

➖ Bus

All devices share one main cable. It is cheap, but if the main cable breaks, the network stops.

⭕ Ring

Each device connects to two others, forming a circle. Data travels around the ring.

🕸️ Mesh

Devices connect to many other devices. It is very reliable but needs many connections.

5. Internet Fundamentals

The Internet

A global network of networks that connects millions of computers around the world.

IP Address

A unique number given to each device on a network, like a home address, so data knows where to go.

Web Browser

A program used to view websites. Examples are Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.

Website

A collection of web pages stored on a server that you open using a browser and a web address.

Note: The internet and the World Wide Web are not the same. The web is one service that runs on the internet.

6. Internet Safety

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use long passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols. Do not reuse the same password everywhere or share it.

⚠️

Suspicious Links

Do not click links or download files from strangers or unexpected messages. They may be scams or contain viruses.

🛡️

Personal Information

Do not post your address, phone number, or passwords online. Share only what is needed with people you trust.
