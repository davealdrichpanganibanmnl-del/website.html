Introduction

The internet is a huge worldwide system of connected computers and devices. It lets people send messages, watch videos, search for information, and use websites from almost anywhere.

Computer networking means connecting two or more computers or devices so they can share information, files, and resources like printers. The internet is actually the biggest network of networks in the world.
